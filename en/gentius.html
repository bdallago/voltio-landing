The problem

Your HR lives spread across spreadsheets

Attendance in one Excel, leave in another, payslips in a folder somebody renamed. When an employee asks how many vacation days they have left, you open three files to answer. And only one person really understands that spreadsheet: the day they're out, the data is out too. Gentius brings it all into one shared, tidy place, so Excel can finally rest.

All-in-one

All of HR in a single app

All of it comes included. The price is set by the size of your company and nothing else.

Time & attendance

Clock in from the app or through biometric clocks that connect with a key and send the punches on their own. Schedules with bands, rotation, tolerances and holidays, and every morning the system spots unjustified absences and opens a case to resolve them.

Leave

Requested, approved and on the record, with templates and multi-step approval flows per sector. Leave flows into attendance automatically and shows up on the team calendar.

Documents & payslips

Payslips and documents go out from the app, with e-signature, WhatsApp delivery and proof the person actually opened them. Signatures store a hash and the IP, so they hold up as evidence, and download links expire.

Comms & climate

Internal board and announcements with read receipts, plus climate surveys that are genuinely anonymous: one single-use link per person, and the answer doesn't store who it was.

Records & onboarding

A record for each person with self-service: employees edit their own data and HR approves the sensitive fields. Import your current spreadsheet and the historical data too: old punches, old leave, PDF files. Onboard new hires with templates.

Payroll inputs & closing

The period-closing screen: hours worked, lateness, absences, overtime and attendance bonus per employee. A closed period is frozen, and the export downloads the sheet your payroll system expects. Configurable reports and audit trail included.

Why Gentius

In the cloud, running in days

It runs in the browser, at the office or from home. It lives in the cloud, so the information is always there for whoever needs it and Excel gets to rest. Import your current spreadsheet and go: setup is measured in days. And the operation looks after itself: nightly backups that get test-restored before being stored, and automatic alerts if anything fails.
